Google put Gemini 4 Carbon on its API at $2 in and $10 out per million tokens, claiming first place on three agent benchmarks.

TOP 3

1. Gemini 4 Carbon lands on the API ✓

  • What: Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to API, business and AI Ultra users at $2 input and $10 output per million tokens until Nov 30.
  • Why it matters: Google claims first place on CursorBench 4.0, WANDR and AutomationBench at under half the cost per task, though the figures are self-reported.
  • Source: blog.google

2. Anthropic launches Claude Meetings, Sheets and Whiteboards ✓

  • What: Anthropic put Claude into Google Meet calls as a visible participant and added beta Sheets and Whiteboards apps for paid plans.
  • Why it matters: Claude now competes directly with Microsoft 365 Copilot and Google Workspace on spreadsheets, meetings and planning.
  • Source: TechCrunch

3. Railway breach ran up $740,000 in stolen AI usage ✓

  • What: Railway's incident report says attackers decrypted environment variables from up to 100,000 workspaces and generated about $740,000 in unauthorized AI charges.
  • Why it matters: Railway believes the attackers wanted AI credentials for resale or model distillation, making API keys a prime theft target.
  • Source: BleepingComputer
