An AI selloff sank the Nasdaq 5.3% on Friday as Nvidia fell 14% and Anthropic slid 9% on its second trading day.

TOP 3

1. AI rout pushes the S&P 500 and Dow into correction ✓

  • What: The Nasdaq fell 5.28%, the S&P 500 3.70% and the Dow 1.93%, with Nvidia down about 14% and CoreWeave down 12%.
  • Why it matters: The Nasdaq is now negative for 2026, a sharp reversal for the AI trade that has led the market.
  • Source: Reuters via Yahoo Finance

2. Anthropic nearly gives back its IPO pop ✓

  • What: ANT fell about 9% to $88.91 on day two, about $6 above its $83 IPO price and well off Thursday's $115 high.
  • Why it matters: Investors hoped the record debut would calm AI valuation fears, and a strategist told Reuters it did not.
  • Source: Fortune

3. Report: Apple is building its own computer-use agent ⚠

  • What: Bloomberg says Apple is developing an agent that runs apps, browses the web and writes code, then pulled the job listing that revealed it.
  • Why it matters: Apple would compete directly with the Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude agents already running on Macs, possibly by late next year.
  • Source: Bloomberg
